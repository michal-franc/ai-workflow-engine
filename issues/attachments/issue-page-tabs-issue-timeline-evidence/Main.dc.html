The issue page: 30 ideas to make it worth opening

The owner opens an issue when an agent asks for an approval or a playtest, and rarely reads it. Today the page is the raw markdown (design, criteria, a checklist per status, comments, decisions) plus a sidebar. These ideas aim at one test: in 10 seconds, what is this, where is it, what does it need from me, what changed, and where did the time go.

Owner: "i am not reading issues that often so we need to figure out how to make them useful again"

1 · Summary and what needs me

5 ideas
01
Needs-you bar Top pick

A band at the top that states the one thing the issue needs from you, in words and as a command: "Approve design → in progress", "Run make playtest ISSUE=… then approve documentation", "Answer question 2". The button is right there, and the agent's wait time ticks next to it. Same item and wording as the Status page's Needs you card, so both pages agree.

Effort S
exists attention.go ownerAttention, NeedsApprovals, playtestsToRun, open question: comments
02
One-line brief

"What this is" in one sentence, next to your original words. Agents already write it: the "In short" line of ## Idea, the first line of ## Bug, and the "Owner, 2026-10-22: …" quote in ## Why. Lift those to the header instead of leaving them 300 lines down.

Effort S
exists body sections · new optional summary: field set at the design gate
03
Path stepper with inner checkpoints

The type's path (feature 9 steps, bugfix 7) with the current step, time in each and the gates you own. Owner approvals inside one status show as ticks too: the RogueTech boss had two (draft approved D-12, destruction approved D-13) during in progress, invisible today.

Effort S
exists workflow.yaml types path, stats transitions ts, D-n dates
04
What you're approving Top pick

At a gate, one card lists what happened since the previous gate: sections written, boxes ticked, decisions taken, commits, scope added. Project status pages at its design gate: Idea, Design and Mockups written, D-2 to D-5 recorded, 10 boxes ticked, plus an unasked-for Origin: null guard. You approve the delta, not the whole file.

Effort M
exists clilog append / check / decide between two transitions, git log on work/<slug>
05
Health chips in the header

The same chips the board and Status already compute: ci-red, needs revision, blocked on, open question, agent stopped. On the issue page they sit beside the title, so a red merge or a stuck agent is the first thing you see.

Effort S
exists markers.go cardMarkers, ciMarkers, blockedOn, needsRevision

2 · Timeline and where time went

5 ideas
06
Life bands: where time went Top pick

One bar for the issue's life, cut by status, each band split into agent working, waiting on you and idle. Local CI took 5h 0m; about 3h 48m of that was waiting on you (approval of the design, then the playtest). That one number changes how you schedule your reviews.

Effort M
exists stats ts + approved_at, "Waiting on" progress comments, clilog gaps (15 min with no CLI call = idle; no heartbeat, dropped)
07
One stream, every source

The agent timeline today shows issue-cli calls only. Merge in comments, commits, local CI runs, playtest results and approvals, with kind filters. Commits are the moments you care about most and are missing now.

Effort M
exists agent_timeline.go, comments, git log work/<slug>, .ci/runs/*.json, playtest: comments
08
Fold the noise

Collapse repeated reads into one line: "63 checklist reads after done, 21:06–23:43" (local CI), "show every minute while waiting" (status pages, bind). The timeline shrinks to the 20 events that changed something.

Effort S
exists clilog, read-only verbs (show, checklist, context, --dry-run)
09
Rework loops called out

Mark each bounce: a playtest FAIL, a correction: comment, a needs-revision, an owner "redo". Local CI's TP5 failed in your playtest, the agent added Last runs boxes and fixed the session; the loop shows as one amber bracket with its cost (11 min agent, 9 min you).

Effort S
exists playtest: / correction: comments, backward transitions, needs-revision marker
10
Owner-wait stopwatch

A running clock on the Needs-you bar ("waiting on you 45 m") and a per-issue total in the header. The CLI config bug waited 26 min for its in-progress approval; status pages waited 5 min. Totals roll up to Status and Stats.

Effort S
exists "Waiting on" progress comment ts → stats approved_at

3 · Decisions and questions

4 ideas
11
Decisions in your words Top pick

D-n as cards led by your quote ("i want it now! i mean we need to build it :D and release"), then the rule, where it was said (lead chat, agent chat) and the sections that cite it. The boss issue's Design headings cite D-1 to D-8; link both ways.

Effort S
exists ## Decisions D-n lines (issue-cli decisions), #slug#D-n citations
12
"You took the recommendation" mark

Many quotes are just the option label: "Page dirs only (Recommended)", "(a) Tall + climbing (Recommended)". Mark those as accepted defaults and keep your own calls bold. Status pages: 4 of 5 decisions were the agent's recommendation.

Effort S
exists decision quote text
13
Stale questions sweep

Show open vs answered questions, and flag body text still asking what a decision settled. The boss issue's Human Input still reads "Open (asked in chat, waiting for the owner): which mockup variant" after D-9 answered it. Wipe Manager's upward map has 8 question: comments.

Effort S
exists question: comments, openQuestion(), "Open" lists in body · new answer link from D-n to question
14
Scope ledger

Out of scope, deferred-to and split-out-of, as linked rows with the target's status. Local CI's D-10 deferred the release gate to ui/status-page-shows-what-waits-to-be-released; the boss was split out of campaign-goal-retake-lost-earth #D-10.

Effort M
partial ## Out of scope, #slug links in body and decisions

4 · Evidence: shots, tests, CI, playtests, diff

6 ideas
15
Screenshot hero and gallery Top pick

For game issues the proof is a picture. Collect images from the body (![…] links), comments ("path in a comment"), playtest runs, the worktree's screenshots/ folder and mockup artifacts; show the best one as a hero and the rest as a strip, before/after pairs side by side, each tagged with the step it proves (MV1, TP3).

Effort M
exists body image links, /p/<p>/files/, .playtest/runs · partial screenshots/ in worktrees · new a proves: tag per image
16
Criterion → proof matrix Top pick

One row per acceptance criterion with its proof: test names, playtest step and shot, CI run, commit, the measured number. Unproven rows are amber. The boss's "alpha ≥ 1.8× the Hierophant's" row shows "690 vs 380 (1.82×), LostEarthBossTests".

Effort M–L
exists checklist ids, tests: comments, Test Plan, session step ids · new AC id on tests and session steps
17
Unticked criteria warning

A done issue whose criteria were never ticked gets a banner and a lint. Project status pages shipped with 0 of 7 ACs ticked. The boss ticked the template placeholder "(list what done looks like…)" and wrote its 8 real criteria as plain bullets.

Effort S
exists checklist, template placeholder text
18
Playtest verdict panel

Steps with your verdict and the machine's, side by side. Local CI TP5: you passed it, 10 machine checks failed. The boss: you confirmed in chat, the playtest-report was not applied, the agent ticked MV1/MV2. Say which happened.

Effort M
exists playtest/sessions/*.yaml, playtest: comments · partial .playtest/runs (not per step)
19
CI after the merge

The first local CI run on main that contains this issue: result, duration, failing tests, and blame if red. Bind-to-localhost: 14b8f6c green in 36 s. Local CI: its own changelog commit d89c1b3 green in 33 s.

Effort S
exists .ci/runs/<sha>.json, .ci/state.json, ci blame
20
Change summary

Commits grouped by step with subjects, files by area, lines added and removed, a link to the branch diff. The boss: 12 commits, 43 files, +2249 −65; bind: 1 commit, 8 files, +174 −18. Size at a glance tells you how hard to look.

Effort M
exists git work/<slug>, merge commit on main

7 · Reading less

3 ideas
28
Since your last visit Dropped

Remember when you last opened the issue (or last approved it) and show only what changed: new comments, decisions, ticked boxes, commits, status moves, each with a "new" dot. "Nothing new" folds the card. Mockup D shows the phone version.

29
Fold the body Top pick

Finished gate checklists become one line ("Testing 7/7"), long sections show their first paragraph, comments fold with "+K more" as on Status. The body stays the source of truth, one click away. Wipe Manager issues reach 11,000 words and 126 comments.

Effort S
exists body sections, checkbox ids, gate section names from workflow.yaml
30
The brief travels

The same 10-second brief (ideas 1, 2, 4) at /issue/<slug>.md, in the approval notification and as the Status Needs-you row's hover. You decide from the notification and open the page only when you want to.

What the real issues taught us

  • The useful parts are scattered. Owner quote at the top, decisions in the middle, the proof in comments, the result in the last 30 lines.
  • Gate checklists are most of the noise. The boss has 45 ticked boxes; only 2 (MV1, MV2) are about the boss, the other 43 are template gates.
  • Pictures live outside the page. 9 of ~1000 RogueTech issues embed an image; the boss's destruction shots sit in screenshots/ in its worktree, unlinked.
  • Comments carry the story. Prefixes beyond the standard ones appear: approval:, probe:, baseline:, correction:, review:, shipped:.
  • Owner checkpoints hide inside statuses. RogueTech's pipeline approvals (draft, destruction) never show as gates.
  • Body text goes stale. "Owner pick: pending" and "Open … waiting for the owner" stay after the answer.
